# RiskLens Policy Corpus

**These documents are ILLUSTRATIVE, written for this project.**

They are *not* real policies from any bank, regulator, or card network, and
must not be presented as such. They exist to give the Stage 8 RAG system a
realistic retrieval corpus, in the same way a real deployment would index a
bank's own internal fraud-operations manual.

They are modelled on the *kinds* of controls such manuals contain — decision
thresholds, escalation paths, SLAs, model governance — so retrieval and
grounding behave realistically.
