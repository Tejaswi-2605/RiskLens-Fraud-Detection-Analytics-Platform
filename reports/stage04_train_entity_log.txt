  Every aggregate is BACKWARD-ONLY: for each transaction,
  statistics over only that entity's EARLIER transactions.
  The Kaggle-winning versions aggregated over train AND test,
  which is legal in a competition and impossible in production -
  you cannot know a card's future transactions while scoring
  today's. Ours gives less lift and would actually work.
  These are ROW-WISE: computable for a single transaction at the
  API with no dataset access. That is what makes them safe to
  compute before the split - nothing is learned across rows.
